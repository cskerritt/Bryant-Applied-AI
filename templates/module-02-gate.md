## Purpose

This gate does not certify that the offer is commercially successful. It determines whether the evidence record is honest, traceable, and sufficient to map a responsible test funnel.

A numeric grade cannot override a failed gate requirement.

## Blocking conditions

Any of the following produces a `blocked` outcome until corrected:

- Fabricated or nonexistent evidence
- A material citation mismatch
- A search snippet or AI output treated as evidence
- Real personal or confidential information in the repository
- A misleading public claim that remains unresolved
- A contradicted core assumption that was not revised or bounded
- Failure to disclose a material limitation or commercial conflict
- Refusal or inability to reproduce the evidence record

## Outcome

## Assignment rubric

The rubric can award partial credit for learning. It does not replace the gate.

| Dimension | Weight | Strong work | Developing work | Insufficient work |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Testable questions and decision rules | 15% | Questions are bounded, consequential, falsifiable, and include evidence standards and stopping rules | Most questions are usable but some are vague or confirmation-oriented | Questions merely ask for support or do not affect a decision |
| Source selection and contextual authority | 20% | Source types fit each question; methods, incentives, scope, and independence are evaluated | Sources are generally relevant but evaluation is uneven | Relies on snippets, summaries, undated marketing, or authority labels alone |
| Evidence and citation traceability | 25% | Every relied-on finding has reproducible metadata, exact location, scope, and status | Minor metadata or locator gaps do not change meaning | Fabricated, inaccessible, materially mismatched, or untraceable evidence |
| Synthesis and decision revision | 20% | Evidence materially updates the buyer, problem, offer, CTA, assumptions, and claims | Findings are summarized but revisions are limited | Treats research as decoration and preserves contradicted assumptions |
| Contradiction, limitation, and uncertainty | 10% | Contrary searches, alternative explanations, and boundaries are specific and honest | Mentions uncertainty but does not integrate it consistently | Omits disagreement or overstates what evidence proves |
| Critical and transparent AI use | 10% | AI use, corrections, rejected suggestions, and human decisions are visible | AI involvement is disclosed but verification record is incomplete | Treats AI output as evidence or hides material AI errors |

## Reflection after the decision

1. Which finding most changed the original offer or buyer hypothesis?
2. Which source initially looked strong but failed the trace or source-fitness check?
3. Which proposition remains a hypothesis, and what later observation could disconfirm it?
4. What did the coding agent do well, and what required human judgment?
5. What claim did the team decide not to publish, and why?
