# Decision 002: Reference research boundary

- **Status:** Proposed for human Gate 2 review
- **Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Gate:** Module 2

## Decision

If Gate 2 passes, proceed to funnel mapping with the following bounded hypothesis:

- Recruit research participants from U.S. NAICS 54 employer firms with 10 to 99 employees.
- Treat firms with 20 to 99 employees as the higher-readiness hypothesis.
- Further qualify for a B2B revenue model, recurring inbound inquiries, at least two people touching an inquiry, an existing system of record, a named process owner, and permission to examine de-identified workflow evidence.
- Describe the fictional sprint as mapping capture, qualification, assignment, follow-up, handoff, and measurement, then documenting a future-state workflow and implementation priorities.
- Use role-neutral buyer language until interviews identify the accountable role.
- Test the 20-minute fit review as the primary conversion. Do not call it validated.

## Evidence boundary

Official data establish that the employer-firm research stratum exists [E-001]. Separate all-industry data show that reported AI use varies by firm size, which supports testing, but does not establish, the higher-readiness hypothesis [E-005]. Peer-reviewed and methodologically described studies support examining qualification, information processes, ownership, handoffs, and measurement together [E-006; E-007; E-008].

The available evidence does not establish:

- A consistent buyer title
- Problem prevalence or urgency in the selected segment
- Minimum inquiry volume
- CRM penetration
- Willingness to buy
- Appropriate sprint duration or price
- A universal response-time threshold
- A causal conversion, revenue, or return-on-investment effect

These items remain test hypotheses. They must not be converted into public facts by persuasive copy.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Undifferentiated “small professional-services firms”:** This grouping includes nonemployers and microfirms that may not have a multi-person handoff process.
- **Software-first positioning:** Research supports process and information design as well as technology, not a claim that a tool alone solves the problem.
- **Famous speed statistics:** The repeated five-minute, 21x, 100x, 70%-ignored, first-responder, and fixed-follow-up claims did not meet the repository’s public-use standard.
- **Numerical outcome promise:** No suitable evidence establishes a result for this fictional offer and segment.

## Consequence

Gate 2 may pass for a low-risk educational prototype after a human peer retraces three evidence entries. The decision does not authorize website coding. Modules 3 and 4 must still define the funnel and testable specification.
